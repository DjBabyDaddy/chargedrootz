Your domain question

Answered

Keep chargedrootz.com. Do not buy a second domain yet.

You asked whether it should be stretchbychargedrootz. My advice: put the stretch site on the domain you already own, at chargedrootz.com.

When the other lanes are ready they live as paths on the same domain — chargedrootz.com/bar and chargedrootz.com/events — instead of separate purchases. That is exactly how my own site runs four business pages under one domain.

Two reasons it matters: you spend nothing extra, and every visit builds the search reputation of one name instead of splitting it across three. If you later decide the stretch side needs its own name, you can add it then and point it here. Nothing gets wasted.

What I need from you

Six answers

Anything dotted in the concepts is a guess standing in for your real answer. Reply to the email with these and I can lock the build.

Your shirt says CNU Stretch. Is that a certification, a studio you work under, or a name you want on the site?
Which city and gym should the site send people to? Your number is a 314 but the flag on the wall is Arkansas.
Session lengths and prices are placeholders. What do you actually charge, and how long are your sessions?
Do you want your own name on the site, or keep it all under Charged Rootz?
Which days do you stretch, and how far ahead do you take bookings?
Should the juice menu take orders, or just show people what you make?

Also — you mentioned videos of you stretching. Send those and I will work the best one in. The four photos in these concepts are yours; I rotated and cropped them for the web.
